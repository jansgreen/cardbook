Cardbook helps small businesses and professionals manage and share business contact information through a modern digital card experience.

With Cardbook you can manage companies, create business profiles, build digital business cards, save other businesses in Book and connect with companies through alliances.

Main features:
- Company management.
- Shareable digital profiles.
- Business presentation cards.
- Book for saved companies and digital cards.
- Business alliance requests.
- Native actions for phone, email, websites, WhatsApp and maps.
- In-app update status.

Cardbook connects the mobile app with a secure REST API to keep information synchronized with the web platform.
