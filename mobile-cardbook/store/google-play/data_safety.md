# Google Play Data Safety Draft

This is a draft for Google Play Console. Review before submitting.

## Data collected

- Account information:
  - Name
  - Email address
  - Username
  - Phone number
  - Profile avatar if provided

- Business information:
  - Company name
  - Company logo if provided
  - Business contact details
  - Website
  - Address
  - Social profile links

- User-generated content:
  - Business profiles
  - Digital cards
  - Presentation cards
  - Notes saved in Book
  - Alliance requests

- App activity:
  - Card views
  - Card clicks
  - Business interactions

## Purpose

- App functionality
- Account management
- Business card sharing
- Contact and networking features
- Analytics and service improvement

## Data sharing

Public digital cards and business profiles are intentionally shareable by users through public URLs and QR codes.

Cardbook does not sell personal data.

## Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS in production and authenticated API requests use bearer tokens.

## Deletion

Users can edit or delete companies, cards and saved Book items from the product. Additional deletion requests can be sent through the Cardbook contact page.
